# Website claim review — October 16, 2026

Scope: new Cloud2e Insights articles. This is an editorial evidence review, not a
claim that every sentence or credential in the articles has been independently verified.

## Findings and publishing decisions

| Topic | Finding | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ESP Directory | Directory explicitly lists CLOUD2E LLC, Level 2, assessment date September 3, 2026. Directory says its listings are validated with the assessing C3PAO. | May describe the verified listing with attribution. Do not infer membership or universal scope. |
| Assessed ESP services | Directory asks customers to obtain the provider's shared responsibility matrix. 32 CFR 170.17(c)(6) requires SSP/service-description/CRM documentation and assessment of relevant ESP services in the customer's scope. | Replace the suggestion that a listing substitutes for a vendor questionnaire with: use the listing as a starting point, then review the assessed scope and shared responsibilities. |
| Phase II suspension | Official CIO About/Policy pages announce July 13, 2026 suspension of Phase II, originally scheduled November 10, 2026; Phase I self-assessments remain. | Core announcement verified. Do not frame this as suspension of safeguarding obligations or automatic removal of every customer's contract duties. Detailed July 14 clause-removal language, taskforce deadlines and current absence of a revised date need direct memo/current-source checks before repetition. |
| Costs | Final-rule regulatory impact analysis states its estimates are derived average assessment efforts, not actual C3PAO marketplace prices, and excludes pre-existing safeguarding implementation costs. | Do not call the article's $105k–118k figure a current market quote or total readiness budget. Require exact final-rule table/value and small/other-than-small, time-period and cost-category labels before publishing numbers. |
| Market prices and savings | C3PAO/remediation/license cost ranges, first-cycle budgets, recertification percentages and 60–90% enclave reductions were not independently substantiated by primary evidence in this review. | Omit from posts. Present only scope-dependent budget categories without dollar or savings claims. |
| FIPS and MFA | Rule distinguishes MFA requirements from FIPS-validated cryptography protecting CUI confidentiality (3.13.11). | Replace blanket “FIPS-validated MFA” wording with separate, accurately scoped MFA and cryptography requirements. |
| Who needs CMMC | FAR/DFARS clauses and solicitation requirements govern applicability; location is not a trigger. 170.17 concerns applicable Level 2 certification requirements. | Qualify FCI/CUI explanations by actual contracts, flowdowns and scope; do not imply every technical drawing is automatically CUI or every federal contractor needs CMMC. |
| Provider selection | Cyber AB separates implementation consulting from assessment, and its professional code addresses advisory/assessment conflicts. | Use practical questions on scope, responsibilities, remediation, evidence and independence. RPO is a designation, not proof of client compliance or a blanket statutory prerequisite to consulting. Do not infer Cloud2e RPO/RP status from article bios. |
